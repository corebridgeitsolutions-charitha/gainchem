About Our Company

About Gain Chem

Quality, experience and customer-focused chemical product solutions.

Gain Chem Industries

GAIN Chem Industries is a leading manufacturing company in the home care cleaning products sector in Sri Lanka. Our manufacturing facility is located at No. 135, Maloes Lake, Kuda Uduwa, Horana.

GAIN Chem Industries is a single-owner company founded by its CEO, Mr. Nuwan Kulathunga, who provides direct supervision over the manufacturing process and is responsible for administrative functions and workforce management.

The company operates a dedicated production plant for home care product manufacturing, where management and employees have acquired extensive experience and expertise in production operations and quality control.
